# Godman OS

**Godman OS is a simple operational workspace for Swedish legal guardians (`god man` and `förvaltare`).**

It is designed to help a guardian keep track of the practical responsibilities involved in supporting another person — not just accounting, but also cases, authority decisions, deadlines, documents, tasks, contacts, and ongoing activities.

## Why I built it

I built Godman OS for my own needs as a legal guardian and trustee for a family member.

I wanted something that could answer simple but important questions:

* What do I need to take care of next?
* Which cases am I currently working on?
* Which permits or authority decisions are about to expire?
* What am I waiting for someone else to respond to?
* What happened in a particular case?
* What income and expenses need to be accounted for this year?
* Do I have the documentation I need?

Existing tools tend to focus heavily on administration and accounting. Godman OS is intended to be more of an **operational workspace for the guardianship itself**.

## Core concept

The application is built around a simple principle:

> **Work from cases, not tables.**

A **Case** represents something that needs to be handled, such as renewing transportation assistance, applying for a benefit, dealing with a housing matter, or following up with an authority.

Cases can bring together:

* Activities and notes
* Tasks and deadlines
* Documents
* Financial transactions
* Contacts
* Decisions and obligations

**Decisions / Obligations** are first-class objects because many important decisions, permits, and benefits have validity periods, renewal dates, or other deadlines that need to be monitored.

The dashboard acts as a **to-do cockpit**, highlighting what currently requires attention rather than simply presenting statistics.

## Life areas

Cases can be organized into life areas such as:

* 💰 Finance
* 🏛️ Authorities
* 🩺 Health
* 🚗 Transportation
* 🏠 Housing
* 📚 Employment / Daily activities
* 👨‍👩‍👦 Family & network
* 📄 Legal

## Financial management

Godman OS also supports financial administration by accounting year, including:

* Accounts
* Income and expenses
* Transactions
* Categories
* Annual summaries

The goal is to make it easier to maintain an ongoing record throughout the year instead of reconstructing everything when the annual report is due.

## Philosophy

Godman OS is not intended to be an accounting system with some guardianship features added on top.

The goal is to build an **operating system for guardianship** that helps the user:

**Remember → Act → Follow up → Document → Report**

The project is intentionally kept simple and is being developed iteratively based on real-world use.

## Language and scope

Godman OS is currently built specifically around the **Swedish `god man` / `förvaltare` context**.

**The application currently supports Swedish only.**

Internationalization and support for other guardianship systems may be considered in the future.
